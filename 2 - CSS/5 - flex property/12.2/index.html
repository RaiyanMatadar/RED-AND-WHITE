Display : flex (Parent Property) Justify-content

The justify content property align the flexible container's items when the items do not use all avalable space on the main-axis (horizontally)

the justify content property can also be used on a grid container to align grid items in the inline direction for pages in english. inline direction is left to right and block direction is downloaded
