------What is JavaScript Hoisting.
Hoisting refers to the behavior where JavaScript moves the declarations of variables, functions, and 
classes to the top of their scope during the compilation phase. This can sometimes lead to surprising 
results, especially when using var, let, const, or function expressions.

	Quick Diagram of it 

     |--> x = 1;
     |    alert("x = " + x);
     |--  var x;

-Hoisting applies to variable and function declarations.
-Initializations are not hoisted; they are only declarations.
-'var' variables are hoisted with undefined, while 'let' and 'const' are hoisted but remain in the 
Temporal Dead Zone until initialized.
