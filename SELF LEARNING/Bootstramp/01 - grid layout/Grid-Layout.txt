col-xxl-1  
• Applies at ≥1400px  
• Makes 1 column layout (12 items in 1 row)

col-xl-2  
• Applies at ≥1200px  
• Makes 2 column layout (6 items in 1 row)

col-lg-3  
• Applies at ≥992px  
• Makes 3 column layout (4 items in 1 row)

col-md-4  
• Applies at ≥768px  
• Makes 4 column layout (3 items in 1 row)

col-sm-6  
• Applies at ≥576px  
• Makes 6 column layout (2 items in 1 row)

col-12 (extra small)  
• Applies below 576px  
• Makes full width (1 item in 1 row)
